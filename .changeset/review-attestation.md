Add `mega review attest`/`mega review check` — a local, git-native, append-only record that a review verdict was recorded against a specific diff hash, and a mechanical way to detect a stale approval (the diff changed after the review). Complements the repo's own mandatory author≠reviewer process discipline with a checkable artifact instead of a self-reported claim.
